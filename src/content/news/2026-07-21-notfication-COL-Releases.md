This blog post is to provide an update on the Catalogue of Life Annual release and the July 2026 monthly release.

Catalogue of Life has traditionally published its Annual release in June. As a long-term supported edition, it provides a stable reference for many users and services. Starting this year, we are moving the publication of the Annual release to December so it aligns with the calendar-year cycle.

Publication of the July 2026 monthly release on the Catalogue of Life portal will be delayed. It will be available on the portal in mid-August. The July monthly release will be available through [ChecklistBank](https://doi.org/10.48580/dgykv) and the Catalogue of Life API.
