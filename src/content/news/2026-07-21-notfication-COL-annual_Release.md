This short notification is to provide an update on the Catalogue of Life Annual release.

Catalogue of Life has traditionally published its Annual release in June every year. As a long-term supported edition, it provides a stable reference for many users and services. Starting this year, we are moving the publication of the Annual release to December so it aligns with the calendar-year cycle.
